# Product Principles

1. The AI starts the analysis.
2. Multiple management problems may exist simultaneously.
3. Attention is not the same as a dashboard alert; it should lead to interpretation.
4. Every important AI claim should be traceable to evidence.
5. Current evidence, organizational memory, professional methods, and industry knowledge play different roles.
6. AI recommendations are not human decisions.
7. Deterministic systems own calculations and thresholds.
8. Review should distinguish interpretation from causal proof.
9. Completed decisions can become organizational learning.
10. Industry examples demonstrate portability; they should not leak into the generic core.
